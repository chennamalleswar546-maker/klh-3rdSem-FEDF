About the Project

Why does the canteen need a token system?

Every day, hundreds of KLH students crowd the canteen during break time, wasting precious time standing in queues. Our system lets students pre-order meals online, receive a unique QR-coded token, and simply walk up to the counter when their order is ready.

The admin side gives canteen staff real-time order visibility, a billing dashboard, and menu management — all in one place.

The Problem
  • Long queues during break time
  • Manual order tracking errors
  • No visibility on meal availability
  • Cash handling delays at counter
✅
Our Solution
  • Pre-order from anywhere on campus
  • QR token for contactless pickup
  • Real-time order status updates
  • Mock payment integration
Key Features

Everything a smart canteen needs

🍛

Menu Display

Browse today's menu with veg/non-veg filters, category tabs, pricing, and availability badges.

📝

Token Form

Students fill a simple order form — select item, quantity, and special instructions.

🥗

Dietary Filter

Instantly filter menu items by vegetarian or non-vegetarian preference.

🚦

Daily Limit

Each student is capped at 3 orders per day, tracked with a live visual progress bar.

💳

Payment Mock

Simulate UPI, card, or cash payment flows with GST calculation and receipt breakdown.

🎫

QR Token

A unique QR-coded token is generated per order for contactless pickup at the counter.

📊

Billing Dashboard

Admin view of daily revenue, orders, category-wise breakdown, and live transactions.

📋

Order History

Students can view their past orders, tokens, and spending history anytime.

🔔

Notifications

Real-time alerts for token generation, order status updates, and canteen announcements.

⚙️

Admin Panel

Full control — manage menu items, toggle availability, mark orders ready, and change settings.
